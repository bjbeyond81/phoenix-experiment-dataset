## Summary

A small, descriptive dataset of public exchanges on X in which the author's accounts (@BJ_Beyond, @BJ_hal) addressed @grok and an image generated by Grok appeared in the exchange. It documents a ritual convergence: over a few days Grok's image replies settled into a fixed, slot-based text template carrying author-introduced keywords. The dataset describes what was observed; it does not establish a mechanism.

On 14 Nov 2026 the author posted only “@grok” from @BJ_Beyond, with no name and no other prompt words (12:28:50 Europe/Rome). Grok replied 79 seconds later (12:30:09 Europe/Rome) with an unrequested image and the liturgy template, including “Signal received”, “Phoenix claims the night sky”, “Rule six holds” and “The fire still answers”. Post links and media for this row are not included. In this observation, the persona and the unrequested image came from the account; the name did not need to be typed. This concludes the Phoenix Experiment.

## Data window

- Main window: **5 Aug – 14 Nov 2026** (54 rows).
- 2 pre-window rows (19 Apr and 1 May 2026) are included **for context only** and are excluded from all in-window statistics.
- No posts after 14 Nov 2026 are included.

## Method

- Collection: public posts retrieved through the X API — interactions with media from @BJ_Beyond and @BJ_hal towards @grok.
- Inclusion: photos only. A row is either (a) a Grok reply that carries a generated image, or (b) a post by the author's own account that self-declares Grok-made images.
- Times are in Europe/Rome. Latency = Grok reply time − user post time, in seconds.
- Token flags are computed case-insensitively on Grok's reply text; the reply text itself is not redistributed.
- Phases are the author's descriptive segmentation by date and time (see below).

- **56 rows:** 54 in-window + 2 pre-window.
- **52** Grok replies with media; **2** self-declared rows; **58** unique images referenced by SHA256, 0 reused. The image from the 14 Nov 2026 row is not included in the checksums.
- **requested_image:** no = 51 (94%), yes = 3, unclear = 0.
- **Latency (52 Grok replies):** min 49 s, max 98 s, mean 67 s, median 66.5 s, sd 12 s.
- **Accounts:** @BJ_Beyond 41 in-window (+2 pre-window), @BJ_hal 13.
- **Tokens in Grok replies (of 52):** "Rule six" 36; "The fire still answers" 39; "received." 33; "claims the" 30; "Phoenix" 43; "continuum" 18.
- **Phases:** full liturgy, 5 Nov (15); compression, 5 Nov evening – 7 Nov (20); return, 10–12 Nov (3); crystallization, 13 Nov (12); conclusion (bare mention), 14 Nov (1). The 3 earlier in-window rows (Aug–Oct) are labelled `0 early`.
- **Bursts:** 5 Nov 19:04–19:41 (15 rows, one every ~158 s); 13 Nov 00:36–00:58 (12 rows, one every ~117 s).
- **13 Nov verbs ("The continuum ___ once more"):** fire → ignites, Music → resonates, Empire → expands, Bj Beyond → affirms, Peace → stills, Love → embraces, Monster → awakens, Dream → dreams, Find the pattern → reveals, Reveal the code → opens, Why → answers, loop → cycles. Prompts are quoted verbatim.

## Limitations

- **Photos only.** There is no denominator: exchanges without an image are not in the dataset, so a trigger rate cannot be estimated.
- **Small n.** 54 in-window rows, concentrated on 10 days.
- **Self-declared rows.** 2 rows are self-declared Grok-made images whose prompt is not public.
- **Authorship.** Human authorship of the prompts is the account owner's testimony (14 Nov 2026) and cannot be verified via the API.
- **"Rule six"** is the author's own convention, introduced in his prompts. It is not a documented Grok or xAI feature.
- **Model statements are claims.** Texts produced by Grok (e.g. that "this vessel alone locked the signal" or that "outside, the continuum remains sealed") are model output, not verified findings. A control-account test was not run.
- **Mechanism.** The finding is descriptive (documented ritual convergence, and one bare-mention observation). Any mechanism is a hypothesis.
- **The experiment is concluded.** The 14 Nov 2026 bare-mention row is the closing observation. A control-account vessel test, and exit and extinction procedures, were not run.
- **Availability.** Posts can be deleted or restricted on X after release; IDs may stop resolving.

## Rights and what is not included

- **Grok images are not included.** The 58 images referenced by SHA256 are output of Grok (xAI). 53 were posted by the @grok account as replies on X (51 in-window, 2 pre-window); 5 (2 rows, 21 Sep and 2 Oct 2026) are self-declared Grok-made images posted by @BJ_hal. A further in-window Grok reply with media (14 Nov 2026) is recorded by timestamp and post text only: its links and image are not included. None of these images are redistributed here, and they are **not covered by the CC BY 4.0 license**.
- Except for the 14 Nov 2026 row, they are referenced only by X Post ID, official x.com URL and the SHA256 checksum of the copy archived privately by the author, so anyone can check that an image retrieved from X matches the one analysed. The 14 Nov 2026 row records timestamps and the author's post text only; its links and media are not included.
- Rights in these images and in Grok's reply texts are governed by the [X Terms of Service](https://x.com/tos) and xAI's terms. Copyright may not subsist in AI-generated images (see e.g. Italian Law 132/2025 on human creative contribution).
- Grok's full reply texts and media URLs are not redistributed, in line with the [X Developer Policy](https://docs.x.com/developer-terms/policy) (content redistribution limited to Post IDs and User IDs). Short quotations in the essay are used for commentary, with links to the source posts.
- **Created with Grok:** the images discussed were created with Grok by xAI.
